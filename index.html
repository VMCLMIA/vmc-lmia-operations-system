LMIA Department – Introduction

LMIA stands for Labour Market Impact Assessment. If Canadian employers are unable to find a suitable candidate for the position they need to fill, they may choose the LMIA process to hire a temporary foreign worker for that position.

First, they need to post the job advertisement on the Job Bank portal and two other recruitment platforms. Job Bank is mandatory and is also operated by the Government of Canada.

So, why do we need to post the job on Job Bank and two other platforms, and who checks this? Employment and Social Development Canada (ESDC) will review the recruitment activities. They want to check why there are no suitable Canadian citizens or permanent residents available for the position and why the employer needs to hire a temporary foreign worker.

Therefore, you need to post the job and complete the recruitment process. Canadian citizens and permanent residents may apply for the advertised position. If you find a suitable candidate through the advertisement, then the employer can proceed with that candidate. If you do not find a suitable candidate, then you need to show the recruitment activities completed, including the interviews conducted and why a Canadian citizen or permanent resident was not found or selected for the position.

After this part is completed, there are other rules for LMIAs as per ESDC that we need to follow and further tasks that we need to complete.

For the advertisement, we first need to know from the employer which position they want to submit. Then, as per the NOC (National Occupational Classification) link, we will identify the NOC code. We need to find the duties that match the employer's requirements, and based on that NOC code, the position assessment will be completed.

Then, for that position, we need to decide the wage and stream as per the applicable requirements and link. After that, we will post the advertisement on Job Bank and two other recruitment platforms.

There are many LMIA streams, but our team works mostly on High-Wage and Low-Wage LMIAs. These are the streams that we will learn as separate topics. For High-Wage LMIA, we need to post the job for 4 consecutive weeks, and for Low-Wage LMIA, we need to post the job for 8 consecutive weeks.

Then, after the recruitment is completed, including the interviews of Canadian citizens and permanent residents, we need to document why they were not selected and maintain a separate document for this.

Then, we will submit the LMIA file as per the applicable stream. After the file is submitted, we will receive a response as per the applicable process and time period.

Keep the main focus on the recruitment part. If we can find a suitable candidate, then the employer can proceed with that candidate.

Once you receive a response from the officer, they will go through the details to understand why the employer needs a temporary foreign worker and why they were not able to find a suitable Canadian citizen or permanent resident. They will also check the information that was submitted.

Based on this information and the applicable requirements, a decision is made on each file.

For all of this, there are many rules and requirements that apply region-wise and in different situations. We will go through all these chapters and understand them better step by step.
